Hairline icon button. `square` is the "›" corner affordance on service blocks; `circle` is the floating contact button (WhatsApp/Telegram/phone).

```jsx
<IconButton shape="square" label="Подробнее">›</IconButton>
<IconButton shape="circle" size={52} label="Telegram"><img src="..." /></IconButton>
```

Pass an icon (SVG/img/glyph) as children. Hover lightens the fill and brightens the border.
